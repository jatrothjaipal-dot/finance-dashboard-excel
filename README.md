# finance-dashboard-excel
** Finance Dashboard (Excel)**

Interactive Excel dashboard analyzing 25,000+ transactions to track revenue, expenses, and profit — built with PivotTables, PivotCharts, and slicers
**
What It Does**

Turns a raw transaction ledger into a live, filterable dashboard answering:

What's our total revenue, expense, and net profit?
How does profit trend month over month?
Which departments and categories drive revenue vs. expense?
** Features**
KPI cards — Revenue, Expense, P/L at a glance
Monthly P/L trend chart
Revenue vs. Expense by Department (bar chart)
Revenue & Expense by Category (doughnut charts)
Slicers for dynamic, interactive filtering

**Skills Demonstrated**

PivotTables PivotCharts Data Modeling Slicers Dashboard Design Financial Analysis

**Dataset**

25,000+ records with Date, Department, Transaction Type, Category, Amount, Payment Method, Approval Status, and Responsible Person.

**How to Use**
Download Finance_Dashboard_Project.xlsx
Open in Excel 2016+
Go to the Dashboard tab
Filter using the slicers

**Conclusion**
 The company maintained a healthy overall profit margin, with revenue exceeding expenses by more than 2:1. Subscription-based revenue is the strongest contributor to income, while Utilities is the largest cost center — insights that could guide future budget and pricing decisions.
